# Early-Stage Founder Collaboration and Trial Agreement

> **Discussion draft only.** This document is a starting point for discussion and is not legal or tax advice. Fill in every bracketed item and have a qualified lawyer review it for the parties' governing jurisdiction before signing.

This Early-Stage Founder Collaboration and Trial Agreement (the **"Agreement"**) is made effective on **[Effective Date]** by and between:

- **Lead Founder:** [Full legal name], of [address] (**"Lead Founder"**); and
- **Joining Partner:** [Full legal name], of [address] (**"Joining Partner"**).

The Lead Founder and Joining Partner are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

## 1. Project and purpose

The Parties want to explore working together on **[application/project name]** and related products, services, technology, content, and business activities (the **"Project"**).

The Parties acknowledge that the Lead Founder created and/or funded most of the Project's progress before the Effective Date. The Lead Founder will therefore serve as the lead founder and have the final say over ordinary day-to-day product and business decisions during the Trial Period described below. The Joining Partner will participate as a trial collaborator and potential future founder or partner.

This Agreement does not by itself grant the Joining Partner any equity, ownership interest, profit share, salary, commission, or other economic interest. It also does not by itself create a corporation, partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship. Neither Party may represent that they can bind the other Party or the Project without written authority.

## 2. Trial Period and review

The collaboration will begin on **[Start Date]** and continue for three months, ending on **[End Date]** (the **"Trial Period"**).

During the Trial Period, the Parties will assess their working relationship, time commitment, skills, communication, trust, product vision, and ability to make decisions together. The Parties will hold a review no later than **[Review Date]** and may then sign a separate written agreement covering titles, ownership, compensation, equity or profits interests, vesting, duties, and decision-making.

Either Party may end the collaboration for any reason by giving seven days' written notice. Either Party may end it immediately for material breach, misuse of confidential information or intellectual property, dishonesty, unlawful conduct, or conduct that makes continued collaboration unreasonable.

## 3. Roles, authority, and conduct

The Joining Partner will perform the tasks mutually agreed from time to time, including: **[brief description of expected role and responsibilities]**. The Parties will keep a reasonable record of material work and expenses during the Trial Period. A record of time or effort alone does not create an equity or ownership right.

The Lead Founder will coordinate the roadmap, repositories, product direction, business discussions, and Project access during the Trial Period. The following decisions require the written approval of both Parties if they materially affect the other Party's rights:

- selling, assigning, or granting an exclusive license to the Project or its material intellectual property;
- taking on a material debt or unbudgeted financial commitment for the Project;
- issuing or promising equity, profits interests, or founder rights to another person;
- admitting another founder or materially changing the Parties' agreed roles; or
- using or disclosing the other Party's Background IP, as defined below, outside the Project.

Each Party will act honestly, promptly disclose material conflicts, and avoid knowingly introducing third-party code, content, data, or confidential information that the Party is not authorized to use.

## 4. Compensation, equity, and expenses

The Parties intentionally leave all numerical compensation and ownership terms open for later discussion. Before either Party performs substantial work after the Trial Period, the Parties should sign a written addendum addressing, as applicable:

- cash compensation, deferred compensation, or a consulting fee;
- equity or profits interest, including vesting, dilution, and treatment on exit;
- any success fee, royalty, revenue share, or sale proceeds;
- credit, title, and treatment of each Party's pre-existing contribution; and
- expense reimbursement and approval requirements.

Unless the Parties separately agree in a signed writing, neither Party is promised or earns a fixed salary, fee, commission, equity, profits interest, revenue share, or success fee under this Agreement. The Project will reimburse only expenses approved in writing in advance by the Lead Founder, unless the Parties write a different rule here: **[expense rule]**. This paragraph does not waive any non-waivable rights under applicable law.

## 5. Intellectual property

### 5.1 Background IP and Existing Project IP

Each Party keeps all intellectual property, tools, code, designs, inventions, content, know-how, and other materials that the Party owned or validly controlled before the Effective Date or developed independently without using the other Party's confidential information (**"Background IP"**).

The Parties will list the Lead Founder's pre-existing Project materials in **Schedule A** (the **"Existing Project IP"**). Those materials, including the application and related materials created before the Effective Date, remain the Lead Founder's property unless the Lead Founder later assigns them to a Project entity in writing. The Joining Partner will list any of their Background IP or excluded prior projects in **Schedule B**.

The Lead Founder represents, to the best of the Lead Founder's knowledge, that the Lead Founder owns or has permission to use the Existing Project IP listed in Schedule A, except for any third-party components disclosed there.

### 5.2 Project Work Product

Subject to the compensation or equity terms that the Parties may later agree in writing, each Party assigns to **[Project Owner: Lead Founder until a Project entity is formed, or named entity]** all right, title, and interest in work product that the Party creates specifically for the Project during the Trial Period or any written extension using Project resources or the other Party's confidential information (**"Project Work Product"**). Each Party will sign reasonable documents needed to confirm that ownership.

If any Project Work Product cannot legally be assigned, the creator grants the Project Owner an exclusive, worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, transferable license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, display, perform, sublicense, and commercialize that Project Work Product for the Project and related business purposes.

This section does not transfer a Party's Background IP. If a Party's Background IP is embedded in Project Work Product, that Party grants the Project Owner a non-exclusive license to use it only as needed to use and commercialize the Project Work Product, unless the Parties agree to different written terms. A material standalone item of Joining Partner Background IP may not be commercially used outside that scope without the Joining Partner's written consent.

## 6. Confidentiality, accounts, and security

Each Party may receive non-public information about the Project, the other Party, users, customers, contractors, finances, product plans, credentials, source code, designs, and business opportunities (**"Confidential Information"**). Each Party will use Confidential Information only for the Project, protect it with reasonable care, and disclose it only to people who need it for the Project and are bound to protect it.

Confidential Information does not include information that the receiving Party can show was already known without a duty of confidentiality, becomes public without breach, is independently developed without using Confidential Information, or is lawfully received from a third party. A Party may disclose information when legally required, after giving notice when legally permitted.

Each Party will use separate credentials where available, will not share passwords unnecessarily, and will promptly report any suspected unauthorized access. On request or exit, each Party will return or securely delete the other Party's Confidential Information and Project materials, except for one archival copy kept only if legally required.

These obligations continue for three years after the collaboration ends. Trade secret obligations continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 7. Clean exit

When the collaboration ends, the Joining Partner will, within five business days:

- stop representing themselves as a founder, partner, owner, or authorized representative of the Project unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing;
- return or transfer Project files, credentials, work in progress, records, and other Project property;
- remove Project materials from personal devices and accounts, subject to the archival exception in Section 6; and
- reasonably cooperate in a short handover of active work and access.

The Lead Founder will pay any approved, undisputed expense or other amount that the Parties separately agreed in writing, subject to any timing and conditions in that writing. Unless a later signed addendum says otherwise, the Joining Partner has no continuing right to Project revenue, equity, or sale proceeds after exit. Neither Party owes the other a termination fee solely because the Trial Period ends.

The Parties will not knowingly make false statements about the other Party or misrepresent the reason for the collaboration ending. This does not prevent either Party from giving truthful information to a lawyer, accountant, investor, regulator, court, or prospective employer.

## 8. Limited post-exit protection

For 12 months after the Joining Partner's exit, and only to the extent permitted by applicable law, the Joining Partner will not use the Project's Confidential Information, Project IP, or Project materials to develop, launch, or materially assist a product that is substantially similar to and directly competes with the Project as it existed or was actively planned on the exit date (the **"Restricted Activity"**).

This is intended as a narrow protection for the Project, not a general ban on working in the industry. It does not restrict the Joining Partner from using general skills and experience, working on unrelated products, continuing a pre-existing project listed in Schedule B, or developing work independently without using Confidential Information, Project IP, or Project materials. It does not prevent the Lead Founder from continuing, improving, or commercializing the existing Project.

For the same 12-month period, the Joining Partner will not intentionally solicit a Project employee or contractor to leave the Project, or intentionally solicit a customer or business contact with whom the Joining Partner had material Project-related contact, for a competing product by using Confidential Information. General advertising and responses to unsolicited approaches are not targeted solicitation.

If a court finds any part of this section unenforceable, the Parties request that it be narrowed to the minimum extent needed to make it enforceable, where permitted by law. If the competition restriction cannot be enforced, the confidentiality, intellectual property, return-of-property, and non-solicitation obligations remain in effect. Nothing in this section prevents lawful reporting of wrongdoing or exercise of a non-waivable legal right.

## 9. Disputes and general terms

The Parties will first try in good faith to resolve a dispute through a direct conversation and written summary of the issue. If the dispute is not resolved within 15 days, either Party may seek relief in the courts located in **[county, state/province, country]**, and this Agreement will be governed by the laws of **[state/province, country]**, without regard to conflict-of-law rules.

This Agreement is the entire agreement about the Trial Period and replaces prior discussions on that subject. Any amendment, compensation arrangement, equity arrangement, or transfer of Background IP must be in writing and signed by the affected Party or Parties. The Lead Founder may assign this Agreement to a Project entity formed to own and operate the Project, provided that entity assumes the Lead Founder's obligations. Neither Party may assign their personal obligations without the other Party's written consent.

If any provision is invalid, the rest of the Agreement remains effective. A waiver must be in writing and applies only to the specific instance. Notices may be sent by email to the addresses below and are effective when receipt is confirmed. Electronic signatures and counterparts are valid.
